Welcome to the Gym Leaders Hub

Explore the prominent Gym Leaders across the legendary Pokemon regions.

Kanto Region

Originating in Gen I, Kanto features 8 iconic Gyms situated in cities across a land inspired by Japan's real-life Kantō region. It is home to classic trainers like Brock and Misty.

Johto Region

Located directly west of Kanto, Johto brings rich mythology and tradition. Its Gym Leaders represent diverse elements ranging from Flying in Violet City to Dragons in Blackthorn City.
